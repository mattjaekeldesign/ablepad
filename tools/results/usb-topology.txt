# --- what is good about this ---
# The EVO 8 is plugged straight into the machine rather than through a hub. That is
# the right call for the audio interface: fewer hops, and the phantom-power budget
# (manual p.22) depends on what the HOST port can deliver, not what a hub passes on.

# --- what is still open ---
# Whether that root port is USB-A or USB-C at 1.5 A. It decides whether 2 or 4
# phantom channels work (EVO manual p.22) and is still an unticked Phase 0 checkbox.
# Not answerable from software — it needs a look at the back of the Beelink.
#
# Both CONTROLLERS sit behind external hubs. The Launchpad is bus-powered and its
# manual (p.5) warns low brightness may be REQUIRED for some hosts to power it at
# all. If it ever fails to enumerate at boot, hub power is the first suspect, ahead
# of the cable.
